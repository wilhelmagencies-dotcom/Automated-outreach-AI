# Nordic Designs: two-meeting sales system, 15.10.2026

Changed from the single 30-minute call in the first presentation, as Carl asked.
The meeting 1 deck ("Nordic Designs Growth Plan") stays as it was presented.
The new system is in the meeting 2 deck ("Nordic Designs: Meeting 2"), which also has
her reviewed email, her before and after images, the website page and today's decisions.

## The flow

1. Email: an offer to style their room. No price.
2. Free sample: one of their own rooms, styled.
3. Meeting 1 (about 20 min): listen and show value. No price.
   - Open (2 min): thank them, agree the time, ask what they hope for.
   - Ask (8 min): listings per year, how many start empty or dated, how long they take,
     what an extra month costs, how they win mandates, who decides on spend.
   - Show (5 min): their own room, before and after.
   - Math (3 min): their cost of waiting, in their own words.
   - Book (2 min): set the date for Meeting 2 before hanging up.
   - Price asked early: "I'll bring exact prices next time, built on what you tell me today."
4. Between meetings: pick one package, put their numbers next to the price, pick the listing to start with.
5. Meeting 2 (about 20 min): the offer.
   - Recap (3 min): read their numbers back.
   - Recommend (5 min): one package and what it includes.
   - Price (2 min): only now, next to their cost of waiting.
   - Close (5 min): "Which listing should we start with?" Send the upload link on the call.
6. Unsure? Offer a one-listing trial: the Listing package on one property, no commitment
   beyond that. Never push and never drop the price.
7. After the trial: ask about inquiries, then the next listing or the Partner plan.
   Still a no: one friendly closing line, mark "not interested", move on.

## Open decision for her

  or a one-time first-listing price.
